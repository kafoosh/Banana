A Guide to Reading Minds & Other Household Miracles

Four ways to dress the same secret. In every one, the premise is identical: you bought a mind-reading course off the internet, and tonight is your first performance. The kit talks, you obey, the spectator watches you fumble — and it still nails their drawing. Every skin runs the same hidden engine; what you're choosing is the costume and how answers secretly enter the app.

ACT 1
The Unboxing
"Congratulations on your purchase." Rules, disclaimers, cover story.
ACT 2
The Induction
The kit itself talks the spectator through choosing a doodle — so a first-timer can't butcher the script that matters.
ACT 3
The Reading
3–5 beats. The magician repeats lines, the spectator answers, the kit reacts.
ACT 4
The Reveal
The kit coaches the magician through claiming the miracle.
ACT 5
The Diploma
"You are now 4% psychic." Resets for an encore with the next spectator.
The two input families. The linear-media skins (1 Cassette, 3 VHS) pretend to be a dumb recording — a NO enters through the rewind button, disguised as "let me re-hear the instructions," and YES is simply pressing play. The paper/web skins (2 GeoCities, 4 Photocopy) use visible controls disguised as error-handling and homework — "RECALIBRATE," "☐ DENIED" — so branching reads as the kit managing your incompetence, never as it collecting information.

1 · The Cassette

c. 1989 audio course, played in 2003 shareware

"Read Minds in 30 Minutes" — Tape 2, Side B, with The Amazing Gerald. The whole page is one audio file in a fake shareware player. Gerald's voice runs the show; the phone never asks anything on screen. This is the one you pitched — and the strongest deception of the four, because a recording "obviously can't respond."

Register
Smooth FM-DJ mentalist, increasingly disappointed in you
Answers enter via
YES = press ▶ · NO = press ⏪ 10s ("wait, let me re-hear that"), then ▶
Deception
— branching hides inside a rewind
Voice
~50 pre-baked TTS clips, run through tape hiss + EQ

2 · The GeoCities Page

c. 1997 personal homepage of a Level 9 psychic

MIND MASTER 3000™, as taught by Mystic Ron. A wizard-flow of beveled buttons, starfields and blink tags. Loudest possible "I bought this off the internet" energy — the branching hides behind buttons labeled as system bookkeeping, and every NO throws a Windows-style PSYCHIC FAULT dialog at the operator.

Register
Overenthusiastic mail-order copy; ™ on everything; no refunds
Answers enter via
Visible buttons in disguise: [YES — CONFIRMED] · [NO — RECALIBRATE SYSTEM]
Deception
— comedy cover, not media cover
Bonus mechanic
Name entry at start → the System chastises you by name

3 · The VHS Course

c. 1991 home-study tape, duplicated too many times

PSYCHIC ACADEMY™ Home Study System — Tape 2: The Mind Read, with Dr. Lance Marvello, Ps.D. The linear-media secret in television clothes: CRT scanlines, tracking errors, a lower-third for a man with a fake doctorate. When a NO happens, the tape "rewinds itself" in a storm of glitch — and Marvello repeats the line slower, with love.

Register
Condescending televangelist calm; everything is said "with love"
Answers enter via
On-screen VCR remote: YES = ▶ PLAY · NO = ◀◀ REW (same secret as the cassette)
Deception
— glitches swallow the branches
Best moment
The copyright screen: "duplication is a FELONY and a CURSE"

4 · The Photocopy

printed 1973 · photocopied 1996 · scanned 2003

Prof. Marvo's Mail-Order Mentalism — Lesson Seven: "How To Know Their Very Thoughts." The driest register of the four: a typewritten booklet that has been photocopied so many times the Professor's face is mostly toner. You work through it like homework; when you fail, the page comes back graded in red pen.

Register
Deadpan 1970s correspondence-course formality
Answers enter via
Worksheet checkboxes: ☐ CONFIRMED · ☐ DENIED ("mark with your finger")
Deception
— homework cover, quietly airtight
Best moment
The graded page: WRONG. in red pen, stamped SEE ME

What I need from you

  1. Pick a direction (or two — the engine is shared, skins are cheap once it exists). My recommendation: build 1 · The Cassette as the flagship, with its boring-player variant as a toggle; add 2 · GeoCities later as a second costume if you want a loud version.
  2. Bless the secret input for the audio design: ⏪ rewind = NO is my strong recommendation (natural gesture, deniable, and Gerald mocking you for rewinding writes itself). Alternatives: invisible tap zones, or long-press play.
  3. Voice: pre-baked text-to-speech clips (~50 short segments), run through tape-hiss/EQ so the cheapness sounds intentional. Engine options in chat.
  4. Reveal style: Gerald coaches the magician to announce the drawing, then the art flips as proof (mocked above) — vs. the voice naming it directly.

Copy throughout is placeholder-grade — one full script punch-up pass happens after we lock a direction. Everything inside a phone frame is spectator-safe: no mechanism words anywhere on screen. The yellow notes are for your eyes only.
