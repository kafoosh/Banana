How to Read Minds

A home course in nine cassettes

Lesson 1
000 Loading

To the student

Read this alone. It is not part of the lesson.

  1. Touch tape 1 in the case. It goes into the player and plays. Do what it tells you. When a tape stops, do the step, then take the next tape from the case.
  2. From tape 4 on, every tape ends with something you say to your participant. Their answer goes into the way you pick up the next tape.
    They said yes
    Tap the tape.
    They said no
    Rest your finger on the tape until it lifts and clicks. That takes under half a second.
    On a tap, the tape lifts as your finger leaves. On a hold, it lifts under your finger. That is how you know which one the machine took.
  3. If it was the wrong one, press REWIND at once. For its first three seconds a tape has only said its number, so nothing is given away. The tape goes back in the case. Pick it again, properly.
  4. Any later press of REWIND takes the tape in the player back to its start. PLAY starts it again. STOP and PLAY pause and resume.
  5. Only the next tape in order responds. The tape always tells you which that is; sometimes it is the red one and tape 8 stays in the case.
  6. Never repeat your participant's answer for the benefit of the tape. As far as anyone knows, it cannot hear you.

To see this slip again, hold your finger on the title plate for one second.
