To the student

Read this alone. It is not part of the lesson.

  1. The wide key on the right is NEXT TAPE. Press it to start tape 1. Do what the tape tells you. When a tape stops, do the step, then press NEXT TAPE again.
  2. From tape 3 on, every tape ends with something you say to your volunteer. Their answer goes into your next press of NEXT TAPE.
    They said yes
    Tap the key.
    They said no
    Press the key and keep it down until you hear it click. That takes under half a second.
    A tap clicks as your thumb leaves. A hold clicks while your thumb is still down. That is how you know which one the machine took.
  3. If the click was the wrong one, press REWIND at once. For its first three seconds a tape has only said its number, so nothing is given away. The tape comes back out. Press NEXT TAPE again, properly.
  4. Any later press of REWIND takes the tape back to its start. PLAY starts it again. STOP and PLAY pause and resume.
  5. The last tape gives you the line that names the drawing. Let the tape finish the sentence, then say it yourself, to your volunteer.
  6. Never repeat your volunteer's answer for the benefit of the tape. As far as anyone knows, it cannot hear you.

To see this slip again, hold your finger on the player for one second.
